Fundermax

Fundermax is an Austrian brand that manufactures a wide range of panels for interior fit-out, furniture and exterior facades.

  • Wood, the raw material, is sourced and processed from sustainably managed green forest reserves.
  • An FSC®/PEFC™ certified company, with 65% of the raw material in its panels being recycled material.

Render Trade supplies Fundermax exterior and interior compact panels.

Products

Max Compact Exterior

HPL panel for facades, balcony cladding, soffits, sun shading and outdoor furniture, protected by a dual-cure weather coating. 2–13 mm thick with decor on both sides, in 5 standard sizes from 2800×1300 mm to 4100×1854 mm, with a flame-retardant F-Quality version.

Podio balcony floor panels

Floor panels with a slip-resistant Hexa surface for balconies, stairs and landings. 16 mm thick (18 and 20 mm on order), in 6 decors.

Max Compact Interior

Solid HPL panel with decor on both sides for wall cladding, furniture, washrooms, hospitals and canteens. 2–20 mm thick, with a black, white or grey core, in 5 standard sizes.
