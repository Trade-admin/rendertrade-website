Parthos

Parthos is a Dutch brand founded in 1968 that has built more than 50 years of knowledge and experience in movable walls, door systems and metalwork, growing into one of the world’s leading manufacturers.

Parthos movable walls offer flexible solutions that save space and make the best use of it, with the added advantages of sound insulation, fire resistance and long service life.

Movable wall systems

Purpose and advantages

  • Efficient use and control of space
  • Freedom of space
  • Ecologically and economically efficient building
  • Multi-purpose rooms

Standards and properties

  • Ceiling-mounted only
  • High sound insulation (35–57 dB)
  • Heights up to 15 metres
  • Wide choice of finishes
  • Fire resistance (60 min)
  • Quality assurance and standards

Products

Palace 80 / 90 / 110S

Acoustic movable walls without a floor track. Palace 80 suits heights up to 3.05 m, Palace 90 up to 4.05 m and Palace 110S up to 15 m.

Palace XXL

Movable wall up to 15 m high for halls and conference rooms, operated manually, semi-automatically or fully automatically.

Transpalace

Double-glazed movable glass wall with up to 50 dB sound insulation.

President

Single-glazed movable glass wall for spaces without acoustic requirements.

Phonic · Progress · Mono

Folding walls. Phonic offers up to 46 dB and Progress up to 31 dB sound insulation; Mono is a simple divider.

Space-saving doors

Threshold-free doors rolling on an aluminium track.
